# Mechanical design source

[OpenFuruta.step](OpenFuruta.step) is the dual-encoder assembly export supplied by Alex Latorre on November 10, 2026, originally named ALatorre_DIYFutura.step. It is a millimetre-scale STEP assembly of editable solid geometry. The original CAD feature history is unavailable.

The nine occurrences include four printed parts and five component envelopes. The motor, AS5600 modules, bearing and coin envelopes are integration references. Consult the manufacturers for their detailed dimensions.

The [assembly GLB](assembly.glb) preserves the assembly pose. To regenerate the component meshes and bed-oriented STLs from the STEP, follow the [model regeneration instructions](../../sim/README.md#regenerate-models-and-print-files).
